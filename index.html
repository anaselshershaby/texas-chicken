TICKET 01 · REACH · CRITICAL

The app is a small channel

The number of App Store ratings roughly tracks how many people use an app. Texas Chicken has about 1.1K ratings in the UAE and 1.2K in KSA. KFC has 17K and 28K, and Albaik has 126K in KSA.

  • One Google Play listing serves both markets: 100K+ installs and 3.8K ratings (4.6–4.7★).
  • The star rating is fine. The issue is scale: too few people have the app installed.
  • The iOS listing is in English only, while 98.5% of Saudi social engagement happens in Arabic.
So what: growth needs a planned app install programme with an Arabic store listing, but only after Tickets 02–04 are fixed, so new users don't leave a bad review straight away.

TICKET 02 · SPEED · CRITICAL

The site takes 21 seconds to show the menu

Both storefronts scored 15 out of 100 for mobile performance. The main content appears after about 21 seconds, compared with Google's 2.5-second "good" threshold. The page stays unresponsive for about 3 seconds while JavaScript runs.

  • Main-thread work: 18–20 s, mostly JavaScript. About 335 KiB of it is unused on the homepage.
  • Server response: about 1.3 s before anything starts loading.
  • The layout shifts as the page loads (CLS about 0.3, where "good" is under 0.1), so taps can land on the wrong item.
  • Accessibility (96–100) and basic SEO (100) are good. The problem is performance.
So what: every paid click that lands on mobile web pays for this wait. Fix performance before scaling search and social traffic to the web.

TICKET 03 · TRUST · CRITICAL

Operations problems become app reviews

We analysed 504 written reviews from Google Play (English and Arabic) and the App Store between January 2025 and September 2026. Of these, 318 were 1–2 stars. Most complaints are about what happens after the order is placed, not about the app itself.

  • Late delivery (26%) and poor service response (24%) lead the complaints, ahead of app bugs (19%).
  • Pickup is broken in several reviews: orders placed ahead are only prepared once the customer arrives, which defeats the point of ordering ahead.
  • Written reviews improved in early 2026 (average 3.5★) but have dropped each quarter since.

«تأخر علي الطلب ساعتين و ما عندهم تعويض على التأخير»

"My order was two hours late and there was no compensation." Google Play review, KSA

So what: marketing can't fix this alone. Set up a shared ops and digital loop: delivery-time alerts, automatic make-good vouchers for late orders, and pickup orders prepared when they are placed.

TICKET 04 · VALUE · HIGH

Customers see no reason to order direct

Neither homepage shows a price or a featured deal. The UAE site doesn't show the rewards programme, while KSA has Tex Rewards in its menu. Meanwhile 13% of negative reviews say offers in ads were missing or priced differently in the app.

  • "Fake promotion": reviewers report pickup prices differing from delivery prices, and ad offers they couldn't find in the app.
  • Every aggregator order costs 25–30% commission, so a direct-only offer pays for itself.
So what: make direct the clearly better deal, and keep every offer consistent from ad to cart. The calculator shows what moving orders is worth.

TICKET 05 · MEASUREMENT · HIGH

Optimising with partial data

The page source shows GA4, TikTok and Snapchat tags. We couldn't find a Meta pixel, product structured data or an app-download banner. Without full conversion data, ad platforms optimise for clicks rather than orders.

So what: in week 1, set up a mobile attribution tool (AppsFlyer or Adjust), server-side conversion APIs for Meta, Snap and TikTok, restaurant and menu schema, and a smart app banner. Every later number depends on this.

Loyalty strategy Sample data

Three rewards to build the habit

Most customers stop after their first order. A customer who has received three rewards is far more likely to keep ordering. So the first reward comes at the second order, not the eighth, and the third reward makes the customer a Gold member.
